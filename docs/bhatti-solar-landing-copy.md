## 3 · WHY GO SOLAR NOW

**H2:** Why Lahore is switching to solar

Electricity tariffs keep climbing, and load-shedding still interrupts daily life and business. A solar system fixes both. With net metering, your panels power your home or office during the day and sell surplus units back to the grid — so your meter runs backwards and your monthly bill drops sharply.

A properly sized system typically pays for itself in a few years, then keeps saving you money for two decades or more. The question is no longer *whether* to go solar — it's getting it done right, with quality equipment and a team that handles the paperwork for you.

## 4 · OUR SOLAR SOLUTIONS

**H2:** Solar solutions we install

**On-grid solar systems**
The most cost-effective option. Your system connects to the grid with net metering, so you save the maximum on your monthly bill. Best for areas with stable supply.

**Hybrid solar systems**
Solar plus battery backup. You save on your bill *and* keep essential loads running during load-shedding. Ideal if uninterrupted power matters to you.

**Off-grid solar systems**
Complete energy independence with battery storage — no grid needed. Best for remote sites, farms, and locations with unreliable supply.

**Commercial & industrial solar**
Larger systems for factories, warehouses, offices, and retail. We've installed 25kW–30kW+ systems for businesses and farms across Lahore, including dairy farms and hardware showrooms.

**Solar tubewell & agricultural systems**
